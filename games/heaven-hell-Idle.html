How to Play Heaven & Hell Idle

Heaven & Hell Idle is an intriguing idle management game where you take control of both divine and underworld realms. Direct incoming souls to their rightful destinations, purchase automated upgrades, expand your operational capacity, and generate resources continuously even while offline!

Controls & Gameplay

Use your Mouse Left-Click on PC or Tap on Mobile to select buildings, assign souls, and purchase upgrades. Automate your operations step-by-step to maximize efficiency and build the ultimate afterlife economy.
