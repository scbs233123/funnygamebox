Build a Keycap Pyramid

3D Building Casual & Idle Typing Game Multiplayer Pets & Upgrades

Welcome to Build a Keycap Pyramid, a satisfying 3D casual simulator where every single keycap counts! Scoop up colorful keycaps by the hundreds, carry a wobbling tower balanced on your head, and construct massive pyramids layer by layer. Personalize your plot, unlock rare pets, defend against Glitch Raiders, and climb the leaderboard!

🕹️ How to Play

Start by collecting keycaps around the map. Chain combos to discover rare Gold Keycaps (x10) and Rainbow Keycaps (x50). Deliver your keycaps to your building plot to watch your custom pyramid rise!

⌨️ Game Controls

WASD / Arrows: Move Character
Mouse Drag: Rotate Camera
Spacebar: Jump
F / Left Click: Throw Keycaps (Defend)
E: Open Shop
1 - 6: Express Emotes

🌟 Key Features

  • Pyramid Customization: Choose from 5 unique pyramid shapes, 10 color palettes, and render your name in keycaps on top!
  • 15 Keycap Pets: Hatch cute pets that boost your keycap capacity, cash multiplier, and training speed.
  • Gym Training: Type display letters on treadmills and benches for a perfect workout rep to gain strength.
  • Defend Your Build: Fight off Glitch Raiders by throwing keycaps and typing shield-breaker codes.
  • Multiplayer Fun: Share a live world with up to 3 other builders, show off emotes, and complete daily challenges.
