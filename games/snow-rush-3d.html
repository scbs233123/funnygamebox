Snow Rush 3D

How to Play Snow Rush 3D

Welcome to Snow Rush 3D! Take control of your sled and dive into a fast-paced winter adventure down dangerous snowy slopes. Dodge rocks, giant snowballs, trees, and obstacles while collecting gifts along the track to set high scores!

Controls & Gameplay

Use A / D or the Left / Right Arrow Keys to steer your sled left and right. Press W, Up Arrow, or Spacebar to jump over obstacles. On mobile devices, tap the left or right side of the screen to steer and tap the jump button to leap.

Timing and sharp reflexes are essential as speed increases downhill. Practice smooth steering, dodge obstacles early, and watch the video walkthrough below for helpful tips and high-score strategies!
